Hi Chloo ❤️

Innaiku un birthday... Aana enakku idhu just oru birthday illa. En life-la romba special ah irukkura oru person indha world-ku vandha naal. Nee en life-la eppo ivlo important aana nu enakke theriyala. Konjam konjama pesinom, sirichom, sanda pottom, samadhanam ainom, neraya memories create pannom... and without realizing it, nee en heart-la oru special place eduthuta. Un kooda pesura every small conversation-um enakku special. Un smile paakumbodhu enakku automatic-ah smile varum. Sometimes nee happy-ah irundha podhum, enakkum happiness vandhudum. Naan always perfect-ah irukka maaten. Sometimes naan unna hurt pannirukkalam, sometimes purinjikama irundhirukkalam. But one thing mattum true... Unna naan romba love panren. Un life-la nee dream panra ellame nadakkanum. Nee always happy-ah irukkanum. Un face-la irukkura andha beautiful smile eppavume disappear aaga koodadhu.

Life namma rendu perayum enga kondu pogum nu enakku theriyadhu... Aana enakku therinja one thing: Namma create panna memories ellam enakku eppavume special-ah irukkum. So, indha birthday-la naan unakku oru wish mattum panren... Stay happy. Stay beautiful. Keep smiling. And please, always be the amazing girl you are for me.

Happy Birthday, my dear Pondati. Nee enakku just "someone special" illa... Nee en favorite person.

En happiest thought. En beautiful memory. And one of the best things that happened to me.

Love you more than I can put into words. Forever yours, With all my heart.
